# Provenance

xsdk is a from-scratch homebrew SDK built from public reverse engineering, mainly Xenia, libxenon and xost, and from results on the console. It holds no Microsoft code, binaries, firmware, keys or microcode (CODE_GUIDELINES.md). It is under the MIT licence (LICENSE), copyright Mena. A few files carry code from libxenon and Xenia under the 3-clause BSD licence; THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md has their notices. The toolchain's patches are under the licences of GCC, binutils and newlib.
